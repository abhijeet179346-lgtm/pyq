# NCERT History

NCERT textbooks for this subject are available from the official NCERT portal. Select the class, subject, and language there to see the current textbook and download full books or chapters.

- [NCERT official textbooks (Classes I–XII)](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php)
- [ePathshala official digital textbooks](https://epathshala.nic.in/epathshala.php?id=Students&ln=en)

This folder organizes the subject. It does not contain textbook copies: NCERT's terms allow textbook downloads for use or reference and prohibit republishing or redistributing the files. Please use the official portal for downloads.
