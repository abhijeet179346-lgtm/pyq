Before you start

  1. The timer starts when you select Start test and keeps running on the server. Reloading or closing the page does not pause it; the paper submits automatically when time runs out.
  2. The paper opens in full screen. Leaving full screen or switching to another tab is recorded and shown on your result{% if test.fullscreen_exit_limit %}; after {{ test.fullscreen_exit_limit }} times the paper is submitted automatically{% endif %}.
  3. Answers save automatically every few seconds and whenever you move to another question. Watch the save status at the top.
  4. Use Save & next, Mark for review & next and Clear response at the bottom, or jump to any question from the palette. Keyboard: A–D choose, → next, ← back, M mark, C clear.
  5. Numerical answers accept digits, one decimal point and a leading minus sign; an on-screen keypad is available.
  6. Answers, solutions and analysis appear after you submit. Answer keys come from the attributed source and have not all been independently verified.
